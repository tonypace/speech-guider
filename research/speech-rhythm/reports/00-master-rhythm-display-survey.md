# Visual Display & Pedagogy of Complex, Unquantized Rhythms: Master Survey

This directory contains the four domain reports and comparative synthesis generated for the Speech Rhythm project:

1. [01-rhythm-games-and-trainers.md](./01-rhythm-games-and-trainers.md) — Highway displays (*Clone Hero*, *Rock Band*, *Paradiddle VR*), commercial drum trainers (*Melodics*, *Roland Time Check*, *Soundslice*), and non-linear rhythm games (*A Dance of Fire and Ice*, *Rhythm Doctor*, *Osu!*, *Taiko no Tatsujin*, *Patapon*).
2. [02-daws-groove-and-circular-sequencers.md](./02-daws-groove-and-circular-sequencers.md) — DAW micro-timing interfaces (*Ableton Groove Pool & Warp*, *Logic Smart Tempo*, *Bitwig*, *FL Studio Red Ghost Lines & Shift Stalks*, *Reason ReGroove*), circular/geometric sequencers (*Patterning*, *XronoMorph*), and hybrid spectral/transient displays (*Serato RGB*, *ReCycle*, *Superior Drummer 3 Tracker*, *Slate Trigger 2*).
3. [03-academic-ethnomusicology-and-paper-notation.md](./03-academic-ethnomusicology-and-paper-notation.md) — Paper and theoretical notations (*TUBS*, *Konnakol/Takadimi*, *Schillinger Ribbons*, *Earle Brown Proportional Bars*), ethnomusicology micro-timing (*Danielsen's Beat Bins*, *Iyer*, *Polak & Jankowsky's Non-Isochronous Subdivision Strips & Tempo-Slopes*, *Benadon's BUR & Circular Plots*), geometric topologies (*Toussaint Necklaces*, *GTTM Trees*, *Desain & Honing's Chronotopological Simplex*), and speech prosody (*Mertens' Prosogram*, *PVI*).
4. [04-rap-flow-vocal-games-and-speech-biofeedback.md](./04-rap-flow-vocal-games-and-speech-biofeedback.md) — Rap flow matrices (*Kyle Adams*, *Paul Edwards*, *Vox Earworm*, *Ohriner's Groove Deviation & Spiral Plots*), vocal games (*Rock Band Talkies*, *SingStar Capsules*), kinetic/variable typography (*MIT Prosodic Font*, *TypoProsody*, *Labanotation*), and speech biofeedback (*P-Center Alignment Blocks*, *Syllable Bubbles*).
5. [05-goal1-post-utterance-feedback.md](./05-goal1-post-utterance-feedback.md) — Deep dive into **Goal 1: Post-Utterance Feedback Displays for Poorly Timed Speech** (Decoupling global tempo drift via piecewise DTW / Smart Tempo from local prosodic errors, diagnosing the 4 core L2 timing errors, and comparing Elastic Tether Diffs, Ghost-Target Overlays, and 100% Normalized Stress-Foot Strips).
6. [06-goal2-live-guided-rap-flow-interface.md](./06-goal2-live-guided-rap-flow-interface.md) — Deep dive into **Goal 2: Real-Time Guided Performance Interfaces ("Rap Flow Game for Speech")** (Solving the split-attention reading trap, P-center consonant lead-in runways, avoiding the Delayed Visual Feedback stutter trap, and designing a 2-Bar Call-and-Response stationary flow matrix).
